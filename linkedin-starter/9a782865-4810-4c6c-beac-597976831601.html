Connecting and Building Content

Who to Connect With

Build out your first 100 connections in three groups:

  • People you have actually worked with (former managers, colleagues, direct reports)
  • Recruiters and HR leaders at companies you would like to work for
  • Industry peers and people in adjacent functions

Starting to Build Content

Start by commenting thoughtfully on three posts a day for two weeks. Posting your own content can come later, after you have a feel for what resonates.
