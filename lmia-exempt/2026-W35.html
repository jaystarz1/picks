This week's LMIA-Exempt postings from Job Bank — Canadian employers hiring foreign workers without needing a Labour Market Impact Assessment. These roles fall under the International Mobility Program (intra-company transfers, CUSMA/CETA professionals, francophone mobility, post-grad work permits, and similar).

How to apply. Applying through Job Bank requires (1) permission to work in Canada and (2) a free Job Bank Plus account with your SIN. To create the account, go to jobbank.gc.ca/plus and sign up — it takes about five minutes. Then click any posting below and use the Direct Apply button.

If you don't have Canadian work authorization yet, treat this list as research — share interesting postings with your immigration consultant. LMIA-exempt employers are often the right fit for IMP-stream work permits.
