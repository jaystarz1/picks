What we do

A human-first job search service

A human-first, intentional job search service for people who want their resume and applications done right — not blasted out by a bot.

We build you a master resume from your real experience. We translate what you've actually done into language employers understand. Then we tailor a custom resume and cover letter for every job you choose to apply for. You pick. We package.

We're not here to get rich off you. We're here to help you find a job, using every tool we have, with people at the forefront. That's why we go slow.

Who we're built for

Intentional job seekers

  • Mid-career professionals re-entering the job market after a pause, pivot, or layoff.
  • Intentional job seekers who'd rather apply to the right jobs well than the wrong jobs fast.
  • Veterans, reservists, and hybrid-career professionals blending full-time civilian work with military service on one resume — see For veterans below.

For veterans

Veteran-owned. Veterans welcome.

Job Scout is veteran-owned and operated. We built the military-to-civilian resume process because we've lived the transition ourselves — and because too many veterans get handed a civilian template that strips the meaning out of what they actually did.

We translate operational experience into language civilian employers understand. We handle hybrid formats for anyone blending military service with civilian work. And we want veterans in the beta — because the process isn't perfect yet, and we need people who've been there and aren't afraid to tell us what they think.

That willingness to give straight, unvarnished feedback is, by the way, one of the most valuable things any civilian employer gets when they hire a veteran. It's exactly what we need from you.
